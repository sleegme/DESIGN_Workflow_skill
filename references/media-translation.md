# Media Translation

This reference governs `translate`.

Translation changes the delivery constraints, not the identity.

## Preserve

Preserve, where applicable:

- brand promise and tone
- logo and key marks
- core palette and typographic character
- visual hierarchy and signature motif
- content meaning and priority
- recognizability across the campaign or product

## Adapt

Adapt to the destination medium:

- aspect ratio and safe areas
- reading distance and dwell time
- interaction versus linear sequence
- print color, bleed, trim, and resolution
- video timing, captions, and motion
- slide pacing and presenter context
- responsive breakpoints and input method
- accessibility requirements

Do not mechanically scale the original. Recompose while keeping the same identity.

## Translation checklist

1. Identify the original design's irreducible identity.
2. Identify destination constraints.
3. Map content priority to the new reading or interaction order.
4. Recompose using the destination medium's strengths.
5. Verify that the result still belongs to the same system.
6. Document intentional losses or substitutions.
