# Generated Image and Photography Guidance

Load this file only for generated images, illustration, or photography-style
work after loading `visual-direction.md`.

## Compose the image

- Choose viewpoint, camera distance, lens behavior, crop, and depth relationship
  deliberately. For non-photographic work, translate these into projection and
  spatial point of view.
- Place the subject against a specific background that explains scale,
  environment, or action; do not treat the background as generic filler.
- Name the key light direction, contrast pattern, time or source quality, and how
  secondary light separates forms.
- Specify materials through observable wear, texture, reflectance, construction,
  and environmental interaction rather than adjective stacks.
- Use focus, gesture, occlusion, and negative space to support the focal point.

## When realism is requested

- Include plausible asymmetry, wear, posture, skin texture, weather, clutter, and
  other context-appropriate imperfections.
- Avoid plastic skin, generic beauty retouching, excessive symmetry, duplicated
  objects, implausible anatomy, and frictionless materials unless requested.
- Keep light direction, contact shadows, perspective, scale, and subject/background
  interaction physically coherent.
- Do not invent logos, captions, or legible text not supplied by the user.

During the one critique pass, inspect the whole frame at thumbnail scale and the
focal subject at detail scale. Repair the highest-impact composition or realism
failure once.
