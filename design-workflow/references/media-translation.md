# Media Translation

Apply this reference to the `translate` route.

Change delivery constraints without replacing identity.

When translation requires visual generation or material recomposition, also
load `visual-direction.md` and only the applicable medium reference. Its compact
direction must remain derived from the source identity and destination context.

## Preserve

Preserve, where applicable:

- brand promise, tone, logo, and key marks
- core palette and typographic character
- visual hierarchy and signature motif
- content meaning and priority
- recognizability across the product or campaign

## Adapt

Adapt to the destination medium:

- aspect ratio, safe areas, bleed, trim, and resolution
- reading distance, dwell time, and content density
- interaction versus linear sequence
- print color and production constraints
- video timing, captions, and reduced motion
- slide pacing and presenter context
- responsive breakpoints and input method
- accessibility and localization requirements

Do not mechanically scale the source. Recompose it using the destination
medium's strengths.

## Procedure

1. Identify the source artifact's irreducible identity.
2. Identify destination constraints and required losses.
3. Map content priority to the new reading or interaction order.
4. Recompose using established tokens and recognizable anchors.
5. Perform the single critique and repair pass required by
   `visual-direction.md`; include fixed/changeable boundary drift in the check.
6. Verify the repaired result beside the source and related campaign assets.
7. Document intentional omissions, substitutions, and newly introduced states.
